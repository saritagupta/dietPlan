7-Day Vegan Weight Loss Diet Plan

For active young women - 1500 calories per day

Plan Overview

This personalized 7-day vegan diet plan is designed for active young women looking to lose approximately 2 pounds per week, focusing on the waist and hip areas. Each day provides approximately 1500 calories with a balanced mix of nutrients to support your active lifestyle while promoting healthy weight loss.

Daily Nutritional Targets

  • Calories: ~1500 kcal
  • Protein: 60-75g (15-20% of calories)
  • Carbohydrates: 170-225g (45-60% of calories)
  • Fat: 40-55g (25-30% of calories)
  • Fiber: 25-35g

Weekly Meal Plan

Monday

Breakfast (350 calories)

Berry Protein Smoothie Bowl

  • 1 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 banana
  • 1 scoop vegan protein powder
  • 1 cup unsweetened almond milk
  • Toppings: 1 tbsp chia seeds, 1 tbsp sliced almonds

Lunch (450 calories)

Mediterranean Chickpea Salad

  • 1 cup chickpeas
  • 2 cups mixed greens
  • 1/4 cup diced cucumber
  • 1/4 cup diced bell peppers
  • 1/4 cup cherry tomatoes
  • 2 tbsp hummus
  • 1 tbsp olive oil and lemon juice dressing

Snack (200 calories)

Apple with Almond Butter

  • 1 medium apple
  • 1 tbsp almond butter

Dinner (500 calories)

Lentil and Vegetable Stir-Fry with Quinoa

  • 1/2 cup cooked lentils
  • 1/2 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1 cup stir-fried vegetables (broccoli, carrots, snap peas)
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp low-sodium tamari sauce
  • 1 tsp ginger, minced
  • 1 clove garlic, minced

Tuesday

Breakfast (350 calories)

Overnight Oats with Chia

  • 1/2 cup rolled oats
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds

Lunch (450 calories)

  • 2 tbsp tahini dressing

Snack (200 calories)

Tips for Success

Meal Prep Suggestions

  • Prepare overnight oats and chia puddings in batches for 2-3 days
  • Cook larger batches of quinoa, brown rice, and lentils to use throughout the week
  • Wash, chop, and store vegetables in containers for easy access
  • Pre-portion snacks in reusable containers
  • Make soups and chili in larger batches and freeze portions

Hydration & Exercise

  • Drink at least 8 cups (64 oz) of water daily
  • Consider 30-45 minutes of moderate exercise 5 days per week
  • Include a mix of cardio, strength training, and flexibility exercises
  • Try activities that target the core and lower body for waist and hip toning
  • Herbal teas can be enjoyed freely throughout the day

Additional Notes

  • Adjust portion sizes based on hunger levels and activity
  • Listen to your body's hunger and fullness cues
  • This plan provides adequate protein for an active lifestyle
  • Consider a vitamin B12 supplement, which is recommended for vegans
  • Track your progress with measurements rather than just weight

This meal plan is designed for informational purposes only and is not intended as medical advice. Consult with a healthcare professional before starting any new diet or exercise program.
